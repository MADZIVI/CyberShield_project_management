Quality Plan

Adheres to ISO 25010, ISO/IEC 27001, and POPIA. Quality enforced through automated pipelines, peer reviews, security scans, and formal UAT checkpoints.

Quality Standards

ISO 25010

Software quality characteristics — reliability, usability, maintainability

ISO/IEC 27001

Information Security Management — mandatory for the SIEM system

POPIA

Protection of Personal Information Act — all deliverables must comply

Playbooks

Reviewed and approved by the IT Security Manager before go-live

Continuous Improvement & Corrective Actions

  • End-of-phase retrospectives to identify improvement areas.
  • Any quality metric falling below target triggers a corrective action plan within 48 hours.
  • A quality backlog is maintained for non-critical improvements, addressed in future iterations.
  • Lessons learned are documented and stored for future project reference.
