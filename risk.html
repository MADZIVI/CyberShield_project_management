Risk Response Strategy

Avoid

Eliminate high-probability/critical-impact risks by changing approach (e.g. strict scope control for R3).

Mitigate

Reduce probability or impact through proactive controls (cross-training for R1, automated backups for R2).

Monitor

Low-probability risks tracked weekly via MS Project and daily standups for early escalation.

Escalate

Critical issues escalated to Project Sponsor within 24 hours of identification.
